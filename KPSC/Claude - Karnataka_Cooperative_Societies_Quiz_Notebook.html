📌 High-Yield Numbers

  • 3 months — registration / bye-law amendment disposal deadline
  • 18 years — minimum membership age
  • 2/3 majority — amalgamation, division & bye-law amendment
  • 5 years — post-sentence membership disqualification bar (S.17)
  • 30 September — statutory AGM deadline (S.27)
  • 50 members or 1/5th — Special General Meeting requisition (S.28)
  • 5 co-operative years — Board term (S.28A)
  • 13 members — new minimum Board strength (2025 amendment)
  • 2 societies — max where one can be office-bearer (S.29C)
  • 6 months + 6 months — Administrator's term ceiling (S.30)
  • 14 days — salary-deduction remittance (S.34)
  • 100 metres — election canvassing ban radius (S.39F)
  • 25% / 2% — Reserve Fund / Co-op Education Fund shares of net profit (S.57)
  • 1977 — dedicated Co-operative Audit cadre created
  • 1/3 membership — threshold to trigger a Registrar inquiry (S.64)

⚠️ Common Traps

  • Section 17 (membership disqualification) ≠ Section 29C (Board-membership disqualification) — 29C is much longer.
  • AGM (mandatory, annual, statutory 30 Sept deadline) ≠ Special General Meeting (need-based, any time).
  • Audit (S.63, routine) ≠ Inquiry (S.64, member/Board-triggered) ≠ Inspection (S.65, books-focused).
  • Reserve Fund (compulsory, non-distributable) ≠ Co-operative Education Fund (separate compulsory fund, different %).
  • Pre-2025 tiered Board strength (9–25) is now obsolete — current floor is a flat 13.
  • Section 14A (compulsory amalgamation) is in the Act's text but was never brought into force.
  • "Co-operative Election Commission" is the old name (pre-2014) for today's Co-operative Election Authority.

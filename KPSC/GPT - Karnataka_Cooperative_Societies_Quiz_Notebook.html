Quiz bank

Tap Show answer, answer mentally first, then reveal.

Q1

Which Act governs cooperative societies in Karnataka?

Karnataka Co-operative Societies Act, 1959 (Karnataka Act No. 11 of 1959).
Q2

Which provision deals with amalgamation, division and transfer of assets/liabilities?

Section 14.
Q3

What majority is required for a voluntary Section 14 resolution?

Two-thirds of members present and voting.
Q4

What is the key approval authority under Section 14?

The Registrar; cooperative-bank cases may additionally require the statutory RBI sanction.
Q5

Which section deals with persons who may become members?

Section 16.
Q6

Which section deals with disqualification for membership?

Section 17.
Q7

Which section deals with nominal or associate members?

Section 18.
Q8

What is the basic voting principle?

One member, one vote, subject to statutory exceptions.
Q9

Which section deals with the Annual General Meeting?

Section 27.
Q10

Which section deals with the Special General Meeting?

Section 28.
Q11

Where does management vest?

In the committee constituted under the Act, Rules and bye-laws — Section 28A.
Q12

Which section deals with committee-member disqualification?

Section 29C.
Q13

What does Section 32 protect through a first charge?

Specified assets of members/past/deceased members for qualifying cooperative dues.
Q14

What is the minimum reserve-fund transfer from net profits?

Not less than 25%.
Q15

What is the Cooperative Education Fund contribution?

2% from the balance of net profits, subject to the current statutory wording.
Q16

Which section deals with Cooperative Education and Training?

Section 57A.
Q17

Which section deals with audit?

Section 63.
Q18

Which Rule is associated with audit fee?

Rule 30.
Q19

Which Rules cover books, accounts and annual statements/returns?

Rules 51–53.
Q20

Which section deals with inquiry by the Registrar?

Section 64.
Q21

What fraction of members can apply for an inquiry under Section 64?

Not less than one-third of the total members.
Q22

What is the normal statutory inquiry completion period?

12 months, with a possible further 6-month extension for recorded reasons.
Q23

Which section deals with inspection of books?

Section 65.
Q24

Which section deals with surcharge?

Section 69.
Q25

What is Rule 5 about?

The subject-matter of cooperative-society bye-laws.
Q26

What is Rule 6 about?

The procedure for amendment of bye-laws.
Q27

What is Rule 3 about?

Application for registration and the registration procedure.
